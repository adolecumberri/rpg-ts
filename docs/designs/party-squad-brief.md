# Squad Card Redesign (2-column card)* **Author:** Lead (EGM brainstorm, DSH)* **Date:** 2026-11-02* **Status:** concepts brief — revised scope after the owner gate (six Goldfish lenses reviewed)## OverviewThe squad cards on the Team page get a real 2-column layout: the 64 px idlesprite on the left, and on the right an identity column with three lines —**name**, **job + job level**, **character level**. The card stays 88 px tall andthe grid keeps two cards per row. Navigation stays exactly as today: Party opensthe party submenu and Squad opens the team page; no route or submenu change ispart of this work.## AnalysisThe six review lenses were run against the original (wider) hypothesis. Thenavigation findings are now out of scope, but the card findings still apply:**High — the sprite math must be stated through `SPRITE_BOX`.** Frames are16×16; `spriteScaleFor(box, frameWidth)` = box / 16, so a 64 px box is**scale 4**, not scale 1. `SPRITE_BOX.squadCard` is 48 today; it becomes 64, thesprite box gets `flex: none` so the row cannot shrink it, and the "3× (48 px)"comment in `spriteFrames.ts` is updated.**High — the selection ring does not render today.** `pixelUI.css` targets`.team-card.pixel-cell.is-selected`, but `TeamCard` emits only`team-card is-selected` (no `pixel-cell`). The redesign fixes the selector (add`pixel-cell` to the root or rename the rule) and verifies the ring visually.Note that selecting a card replaces the grid with `TeamData`, so the ring isonly visible transiently — the redesign must state where it matters (e.g. keepthe selected card ringed when returning from a detail view).**Medium — the width budget is tight at 2 cards per row.** Canvas 320 px, card~146 px (~142 px inside borders); a 64 px sprite leaves ~70 px for three lines.The owner accepted 64 px, so the identity lines use the smallest type sizes,`white-space: nowrap` + ellipsis per line, and a zeroed top margin; the risk of"Adventurer Lv.1" clipping is documented as accepted, with the 56 px fallbacknoted.**Medium — `.team-data-identity` is not the block to reuse.** It is a `flex: 1`panel column with `margin-top: var(--s3)`, 12 px type and absolutely-positionedchildren. The card gets a new `.team-card-identity` (gap + type + coloursmirroring the panel), reusing only `.team-data-lv` for the level line.**Medium — the pending badge detaches.** `.team-card-badge` is absolute inside`.battle-card-sprite`, so a left-column sprite drags it into the card's middle.It moves to the `.team-card` corner.**Medium — "job + job level" is undefined for jobless characters.**`heldJobOf` is undefined for The Player and `jobLevelOf` falls back to the`'none'` bucket returning 1. Formats: job line = `${job.title} · Lv ${jobLevel}`when a job is held, otherwise `The Player`; level line = `Lv ${character.experience.level}`always. Pending level-up characters keep the ↑ badge.**Low — no overflow, no pagination needed.** The active party cap is 6 → 3 rows= 280 px inside a scrollable grid (~332 px); `.team-grid` already scrolls, so noarrows.**Low — the "THE SQUAD" header stays.** It is the page title and the only "tap acharacter" hint; removing it buys less than one card row. Dead `.squad-card*`selectors are not part of this change.**Low — verification has no DOM harness.** `test/` is engine-only; verificationis a manual 320×640 walkthrough with the longest names/titles/levels, checkingthe 64 px sprite, no crop, no text overflow and the badge corner.## Goals vs. Non-Goals- **In-scope goals:** 2-column card (sprite | identity) at 88 px height; 64 px  sprite box via `SPRITE_BOX.squadCard` (scale 4); identity lines name / job +  job level / level with ellipsis; new `.team-card-identity` styles; badge on  the card corner; visible selection ring; jobless fallback format.- **Out-of-scope non-goals:** Party/navigation changes (submenu and Squad stay),  removing THE SQUAD header, pagination arrows, roster cap changes, new tests.## Alternatives Weighed1. **58 px sprite / two lines** — rejected: the owner wants 64 px and three   lines; the risk is accepted with ellipsis and documented.2. **One wide card per row** — rejected: the 2-column grid stays.3. **Reuse `.team-data-identity`** — rejected: it carries panel spacing and   absolutely-positioned children; a dedicated identity block is clearer.4. **Remove THE SQUAD header** — rejected: it saves less than a row and loses   the only hint text.5. **Navigation restructure (Party → squad, Squad removed)** — raised in the   original ask, rejected at the owner gate: orphaned Inventory/Misiones and an   ambiguous Back target.## Key Decisions & Staging1. `SPRITE_BOX.squadCard = 64`; `SPRITE_BOX`-driven scale (4×); `flex: none`.2. `.team-card` becomes a 2-column flex row (sprite | identity); unchanged   height 88 px; grid unchanged (2 columns).3. New `.team-card-identity` with three lines; formats and jobless fallback as   above; per-line ellipsis.4. Badge anchored to `.team-card`; selection ring selector fixed.5. Verification: typecheck, lint, build, and a 320×640 headless screenshot of   the team grid with the longest data.## Implemented outcome (supersedes the first gate decision)After the owner review of the shipped cards, the scope was raised again andshipped as follows:- **Party** opens the party panel **and** the place page's central container  switches from the place hero to the inline squad (same TeamCard grid as the  team page).
